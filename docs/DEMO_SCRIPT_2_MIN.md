# SmartShelf AI — 2-Minute Demo Script

Total time: ~2 minutes. Read at a relaxed pace. Brackets indicate actions.

---

## Opening — The Problem (30 seconds)

"A gas station owner in Tel Aviv starts every morning the same way: walking the shelves, checking what ran out, trying to remember what sold fast last week, and calling suppliers based on gut feel.

The result? Red Bull is out of stock on a Friday evening. A case of hummus expires in the back. The manager spent forty minutes on ordering decisions that a spreadsheet could handle — if he had time to build one.

SmartShelf AI gives that owner the spreadsheet they never had time to build, with the intelligence layer on top."

---

## Product Walkthrough (90 seconds)

### Dashboard [navigate to Dashboard]

"This is what greets the owner every morning. Six numbers that matter: how many products are at risk of running out, how many reorder suggestions are waiting, and the estimated cost of acting on all of them today.

Below that, sales by category over the last thirty days — and the market context. Today: [point to panel] weather, upcoming holidays, local events. The system uses these signals to adjust which products it flags as urgent."

### Recommendations [navigate to Smart Reorder]

"Here's the core workflow. The system has ranked every product by stockout risk, sales velocity, and margin. This one — [point to top HIGH urgency card] — is flagged HIGH. The explanation tells you exactly why: stock is low, it sells fast, and a competitor nearby just ran out of the same product, so demand is about to spike.

The manager can edit the quantity [click quantity field], or just click Approve. [click Approve]

Done. That decision took four seconds."

### Approved Orders [navigate to Approved Orders]

"The approved item lands here, grouped by supplier, with the unit cost, quantity, and total already calculated. The manager can export this as a CSV for the supplier, or print it. [point to Export CSV and Print buttons]

One purchase order, ready in under five minutes."

---

## Closing (15 seconds)

"SmartShelf AI replaces the morning shelf walk, the phone call to the supplier, and the expiry-date guessing game. The goal after the hackathon is to put this in every gas station and mini-market in Israel — starting with a three-store pilot next month."
