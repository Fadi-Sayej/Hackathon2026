## PAGE 1 — Quick start (one page, print it)

### What this is

A daily list of things worth fixing in your shop, worked out from your own POS export and from the
prices at nearby shops.

### Your ten minutes each morning

1. **Send us the stock export** — the same file, once a day. About 30 seconds.
2. **Open the app** and go to **Today**.
3. **Work down the list from the top.** It is ordered by how much money each item is worth. The
   first few are worth more than all the rest put together.
4. **Press Done** when you have dealt with something, or **Dismiss** if it is not worth doing.

That's it. You do not need to read the whole list.

### The three screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your action list. Start here every morning. |
| **Prices** | How your prices compare to Dor Alon, Rami Levy and Shufersal. |
| **Expiry** | Record expiry dates when goods arrive, so we can warn you before they expire. |

### When something is wrong

**Please press Dismiss and tell us why.** If the data is wrong, choose *"The data is wrong"* — that
is genuinely the most useful thing you can do for us. It tells us which suggestions to stop showing
you.

## PAGE 2 — What this does and does not do (print it)

Written plainly, because trust matters more to us than looking clever.

### ✅ What it does well

- **Finds products you sell for less than they cost you.** Straight from your own prices, and
  reliable.
- **Compares your prices to nearby shops.** About 2,000 of your products are matched by barcode.
- **Shows where your shelf price and your Wolt price disagree.**
- **Warns you about expiry** — but only for products where you have recorded an expiry date.

### ⚠️ What it cannot do yet

- **It cannot tell you what is about to run out.** You told us the stock counts in the system are
  often wrong, so we deliberately do not make predictions from them. Nothing in this app will say
  "you are running out".
- **It does not know what sells fastest.** Once you send us the sales report, it will.
- **Competitor prices are not live.** Each one shows when it was last seen — some are a few days
  old, some a few months. **Check before you act on an old one.** We would rather show you the date
  than pretend it is today's price.
- **It does not order anything for you.** Every decision stays yours.

### About the numbers

Where it says *"₪12 per sale"*, that is what the item is worth **each time it sells** — not a total.
We do not yet know how many you sell, so we do not guess.

Some cost prices in your system look like the price of a whole case rather than one unit. Where we
spotted that, we put the item under **"Data to fix"** instead of claiming you are losing money on it.

### Say these out loud

- **"Stock counts are wrong, so we removed anything that depends on them."** He raised this himself.
  Showing him we listened is worth more than any feature.
- **"The strongest thing here is prices and margin, because those come from numbers we trust."**
- **"Tell us what's wrong. That's the point of the trial."**

### Get these before leaving

- [ ] **Roughly how much they throw away each month** — the baseline. Without a "before" number,
      nothing we report at the end can be attributed to us. **Do not leave without this.**
- [ ] Who to contact day to day, and the best number.
- [ ] Whether staff will use it, or only the manager — decides whether the interface moves to
      Hebrew/Arabic.
- [ ] Confirm the daily export routine, and watch them do it once.

### Show, don't tell

Open **Today**, point at the first item, and ask *"is this worth fixing?"* Their answer to that one
question tells you more than the whole rest of the meeting.

### Do not

- Do not promise stockout prediction, reorder quantities, or anything velocity-based until the sales
  report is in and working.
- Do not show the Planogram — it is hidden for a reason.
- Do not present a competitor price without its date.
